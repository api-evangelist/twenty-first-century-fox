# Twenty-First Century Fox

Twenty-First Century Fox was an American multinational mass media corporation that operated film, television, and cable assets before being largely acquired by The Walt Disney Company in 2019. Its remaining broadcasting and news assets continued under Fox Corporation. The company no longer exists as an independent entity.
